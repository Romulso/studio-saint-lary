School holidays: Saturday to Saturday. During the French autumn, Christmas, February and summer holidays, stays start and end on a Saturday. The rest of the year, you choose your own dates.

On departure day, the flat must be vacated by 10 am, to leave time to prepare it for the next guests, who arrive from 3 pm.

End-of-stay cleaning is optional. If you would rather not take it, please contact us before you arrive and we will explain what is expected so the flat is left as you found it.

The tourist tax is added to the price of the stay: €1.90 per adult per night. This is the rate set by the commune of Saint-Lary-Soulan for rentals rated 3 stars. Under-18s are exempt. We collect it on behalf of the commune and pass it on in full: none of it is ours. For two adults over a week, that comes to €26.60.

The security deposit is taken online, through Swikly. Before you arrive, we send you a link where you register your bank card for €300. Nothing is charged and your card limit is not blocked. If the flat is left in good condition, the deposit expires on its own a few days after you leave, with nothing for you to do. If anything is damaged, we tell you with photos before any deduction, which is limited to the actual cost of repair or replacement.

How booking works

You tell us your dates, using the calendar above, on WhatsApp or by phone. As soon as we receive them, we place an option: your dates are held and no longer show as available to anyone else while we talk. We then confirm, a rental contract is drawn up, and the option becomes a firm booking once the advance payment is received.

There is no middleman, no platform fee and no commission. You deal directly with Karine and Romuald, who answer in person.
