Where it is

The Royal Milan residence is on chemin de Vielle-Aure in Saint-Lary-Soulan, directly opposite the thermal baths. It is an unusual position in the resort: the ski lifts are two hundred metres away, the baths one hundred and fifty, and the village centre four hundred. Everything is walkable, including with skis on your shoulder.

It has forty-five flats, which makes it a human-scale residence rather than a large complex: the shared spaces stay quiet, even in high season.

Wellness, at no extra cost

The sauna is free for guests. There is nothing to pay and nothing to book: it is included in the rental. The fitness room works the same way. In summer, the heated outdoor pool opens — that is what sets this apart from an ordinary village rental when you come with children.

Opening hours

Opening hours for the sauna, gym and pool are set by the residence, posted in the entrance hall, and vary with the season: the outdoor pool only opens in summer. We confirm the current hours the day before you arrive, together with the entry codes — the only way not to quote you a time that has changed between booking and stay.

Storage, and the rest

A ski locker saves carrying gear back up every evening, and a locked bike room solves the question in summer. There is a car park, a shared laundry, and Wi-Fi throughout the building, flats included.

The shared lounge, with its stone fireplace, bar, pool table and table football, matters on a long stay or a rainy day: somewhere to go that is neither the flat nor a café.
