At the Royal Milan residence

A human-scale residence of 45 flats, chemin de Vielle-Aure, facing the thermal baths, with a wellness area free for guests.

  • Sauna and jacuzzi, free access
  • Heated outdoor pool in summer
  • Fitness room
  • Lounge with bar, billiards and table football
  • Ski locker and bike storage
  • Car park
  • Shared laundry and Wi-Fi

Inside the flat

  • 9 m² mountain-view balcony
  • Dishwasher
  • Hob, microwave, fridge
  • Coffee maker and toaster
  • Television
  • Electric heating
Good to know

First floor, no lift. Wi-Fi is available in the common areas, not inside the flat. Beds are in the living area; there is no separate bedroom. Dogs are welcome in summer only, under conditions (ask us); no pets in winter.

Loudenvielle and the Louron valley

35 minutes away

The neighbouring valley, and the best idea for a rainy day as much as for a summer's day. A large mountain lake, a well-known thermal spa, parks designed for children, and one of the widest mountain-bike areas in the Pyrenees. Enough to fill a whole day without touching the car again once you are there.
