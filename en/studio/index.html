Where you sleep

The flat sleeps three. The beds are in the living area: there is no separate bedroom, and that is the first thing to know before booking. For a couple, a couple with a child, two friends or someone travelling alone, the space is comfortable. For three adults who do not know each other, it is worth knowing in advance.

The kitchenette

It is fully equipped, which is unusual for this size: dishwasher, a two-ring ceramic hob, a combination microwave-oven, fridge, food processor, two coffee makers — one of them a capsule machine —, kettle and toaster. Over a week — and even more over a long stay — cooking here makes a real difference to the budget. The village shops are four hundred metres away and the market is on Saturday mornings.

The balcony

Nine square metres — a third of the indoor floor area, added outside. It faces due south, over the mountains and the Aure valley. In summer, that is where the end of the day happens; in winter the afternoon sun lands on it, and it also serves to dry out whatever comes back from the slopes.

What the flat is not

We would rather say this before than after. The flat is on the first floor with no lift. There is no separate bedroom. Dogs are welcome in summer only, under conditions; no pets in winter.

Rated 3 stars, Confort + label

The flat has been officially rated 3 stars by the Saint-Lary-Soulan tourist office, and awarded its Comfort Quality label at the Confort + level, 4 diamonds out of 5. This local label follows an on-site visit checking bedding, furniture, bathroom, kitchen and the services offered.

Linen: bring your own

Sheets, duvet covers, pillowcases and towels are not provided. Duvets and pillows are provided, so you only need to bring the linen that goes on them, for up to three beds. The kitchen, on the other hand, is fully equipped: crockery, utensils, coffee maker, kettle and toaster.
